FAQ

Development Questions We Hear Often

How long does a website project take?
A simple business website can take a few weeks. Larger websites, apps, or custom systems take longer depending on features, content, and complexity.
Do you build custom websites or use templates?
We can do both depending on your budget and goals. For serious business growth, we recommend a custom structure and design approach.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We can review your current website, identify what is not working, and rebuild it with better structure, design, performance, and conversion flow.
Do you build mobile apps for both iOS and Android?
Yes. We can build iOS, Android, and cross-platform apps depending on your project needs.
Can you help with website content too?
Yes. We can help with page structure, copy direction, calls-to-action, and content planning.
Do you provide maintenance after launch?
Yes. We can provide support, updates, improvements, backups, monitoring, and future feature development.
